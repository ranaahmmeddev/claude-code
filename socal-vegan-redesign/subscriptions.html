    Good to know

    What happens if…

    …I don't choose new meals?

    Your order still goes through. Last week's picks repeat. If you have open slots after the cutoff, Sharon fills only those with Chef's Choice meals from that week's menu, exactly enough to reach your plan's count.

    …my payment fails?

    We email and text you right away and try once more Friday around 8:00 AM. If that also fails, that week isn't produced and your subscription picks up again the next week. Update your card in your account.

    …a meal sells out?

    It's marked "Not available this week" and can't be picked. Orders already paid for are kept as is.

    …I want extra meals one week?

    Add à la carte meals any time ($18 each). They're a separate order and don't change your subscription.

    Subscription FAQ

    Before you sign up.

    Anything else, email hello@thesocalvegan.com. We answer Mon–Fri, 8 AM – 5 PM.

    When am I charged?+

    Every Thursday at 2:00 PM Pacific, right at the weekly cutoff. That charge covers the meals delivered the following Monday.

    Do I have to pick meals every week?+

    No. Your previous picks carry forward. Change them any week between Tuesday 8 AM and Thursday 2 PM.

    Can I pause instead of cancel?+

    Use Skip Week for any week you don't need meals. You can skip as many individual weeks as you like, one at a time.

    Can I choose my delivery day?+

    Delivery is always Monday morning, about 4:00–7:30 AM. There's no date to pick.

    Can I get more than one of the same meal?+

    Yes. Choose any quantity of any meal, up to your plan's total, while it's available.

    What if something's wrong with my order?+

    Email us as soon as possible with your name, order number and a photo if something's damaged. Once verified, we'll make it right with a replacement, credit or refund.

    Subscription terms, in short

    • Subscriptions renew weekly until you skip or cancel.
    • Meal selections, skips and cancellations close Thursday at 2:00 PM PT for the upcoming Monday.
    • After the cutoff and charge, that week's order is committed and non-refundable, except for verified order or quality issues.
    • Cancel by emailing hello@thesocalvegan.com. Late requests take effect the following week.
    • You're responsible for an accurate address, gate or access instructions, and refrigerating meals promptly after delivery.
