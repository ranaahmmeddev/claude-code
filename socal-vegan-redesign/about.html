Founder & cook

Hi, I'm Sharon.

Vegan eats specialist. Recipe whisperer. Spice fanatic.

I'm the face, cook, recipe tinkerer, and plant-powered foodie behind The SoCal Vegan.

What started from a love of good food, plants, flavor, and the Southern California lifestyle has grown into something I'm incredibly proud to share with my community in North County San Diego.

I believe eating plant-based shouldn't mean settling for boring salads or spending your entire Sunday meal-prepping.

It should be colorful, satisfying, flavorful, convenient, and something you actually look forward to eating.

That's why I create rotating weekly menus filled with thoughtfully prepared vegan meals designed to make your busy week a little easier.

— Sharon King, Carlsbad, CA

Our mission

Make delicious plant-based meals easy and convenient for busy people in North County San Diego.
